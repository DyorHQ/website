Self-custodial by design

Your wallet. Your keys.

DyorHQ is a decentralized, permissionless app. It never holds your keys or your funds — every action is signed on your device.

Your keys live on your device, in the Secure Enclave. DyorHQ can't move your funds — only you can sign.

Non-custodial keys

Sign in with a passkey, email, or bring your own wallet by recovery phrase. Face ID gates every signature.
