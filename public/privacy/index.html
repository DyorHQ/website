Privacy Policy

Last updated: [date of publication]

This policy explains what personal data DyorHQ collects through the DyorHQ iOS app, the DyorHQ web app and this website, why, who processes it for us, and the choices you have. DyorHQ is self-custodial: we never hold your private keys or your funds.

Who we are

The controller of your personal data is [legal entity name], [registered address], [company number]. Contact: team@dyorhq.fun.

What we collect

  • Waitlist email. If you join the waitlist on this website, we keep your email address to send launch updates.
  • Sign-in details. If you sign in with email, the email address is verified by our authentication provider (Privy) and we store the link between your email and your wallet address.
  • Wallet address. Your public wallet address identifies your account in our backend.
  • Profile. The handle, display name, bio and avatar image you choose to add.
  • App activity. A record of actions you take in the app (for example a swap, send, launch, perps order or bridge), including the transaction hash, the USD value and fee, and the time, so your activity history and notifications work across devices.
  • Preferences. Price alerts, watchlist, notification settings and other app settings.
  • Sessions. When you sign in and out, and the platform (iOS or web).
  • Push notification token. If you allow notifications, your device's push token.
  • Social features. Posts, comments, reactions and follows you make in the app.
  • Security data. To protect email sign-in against password guessing we keep short-lived, rate-limiting records that may include your IP address.
  • Moments media. Photos and videos you publish as a Moment are stored and pinned to IPFS and are public.

We do not collect your private key, recovery phrase or password. Email & Password wallets are derived on your device.

Public blockchain data

Transactions you make are recorded on public blockchains (Monad and, when you bridge, other networks). This data is public and permanent, and neither we nor anyone else can delete it.

Why we use it

  • To provide the app and its features (account, activity, alerts, notifications, profiles, social features) — performance of our contract with you.
  • To keep accounts secure and prevent abuse — our legitimate interests.
  • To send waitlist and launch updates you asked for — your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal data and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

Who processes it for us

  • Supabase (database, file storage, backend functions)
  • Privy (email sign-in)
  • Cloudflare (web app hosting)
  • Replit (website hosting)
  • Pinata (IPFS pinning for Moments media)
  • Apple (push notifications)

When you use a feature that talks to a third-party service (for example swap quotes, perps trading, bridging or market data), that service receives the request, including your wallet address where needed. [List the data transfer mechanisms that apply for international transfers.]

How long we keep it

We keep account data while your account exists. Security records are kept for a short time (from hours to a day). Waitlist emails are kept until you unsubscribe or we launch and no longer need them. [Confirm retention periods.]

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account in the app: Profile → Delete Account removes your profile and app data from our backend.
  • Ask us for access, correction, deletion, restriction or a copy of your data, or object to its use, by emailing team@dyorhq.fun.
  • Withdraw consent to marketing emails at any time.
  • You may complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

DyorHQ is not intended for anyone under 18.

Changes

We will post any change to this policy on this page and update the date above.
