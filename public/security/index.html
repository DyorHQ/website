Security & bug reports

DyorHQ is a self-custodial wallet with smart contracts on mainnet, so we take reports seriously. If you believe you have found a security vulnerability in the DyorHQ app, web app, backend or contracts, please tell us privately.

How to report

  • Email team@dyorhq.fun with the subject "Security report".
  • Include what is affected, how to reproduce it, the impact, and any proof of concept.
  • Do not include anyone's private key or recovery phrase.

Please

  • Give us reasonable time to fix the issue before you disclose it.
  • Test only against your own accounts and funds. Do not access other people's data or funds, and do not disrupt the service.
  • Do not use social engineering, phishing or physical attacks.

We will acknowledge your report, keep you updated, and credit you if you wish once the issue is fixed. [Add bug bounty terms here if DyorHQ offers one.]
