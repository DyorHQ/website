Before you write

  • A transaction is "sent" but not confirmed. Open it with View in the app, or search your wallet address on Monadscan. Do not send it again until you know the first one failed.
  • A bridge has not arrived. Bridges settle on the bridge provider's side and can take a while. Check your balance on the destination network before trying again.
  • You can't sign in with email. Make sure you use the same email and password you signed up with. Too many attempts are paused for a few minutes.
